Core message

From time series to the digital twin in which decisions have a causal effect. Built on a data platform that already stands.

P8 · Interplay and coordination potential new

"What do we lose through separate operational management, and what do we gain through coordinated action?"

This means the joint optimum of the local energy system. Wind farm, battery, electrolyzer, energy consumers and marketing are not looked at in isolation. P8 creates data-based clarity on which the parties can base their cooperation: demands, capacities, restrictions and commitments become traceable.

To be included:

  • Weather and generation forecasts
  • Electricity prices and marketing schedules
  • Hydrogen demand and offtake obligations
  • Storage levels, capacities and technical operating limits
  • Grid restrictions
  • Field work, bat protection shutdowns and other operating rules
P8 in view A · technical

What is produced?

A verifiable model of the dependencies and the room for action, plus a quantified coordination gap.

How is it produced?

  1. Bring together time series, events, schedules and context information.
  2. Identify time-lagged dependencies and operating regimes.
  3. Check the relations found against the physical topology, balances and domain knowledge.
  4. Model dynamics, levers and constraints.
  5. Compute coordinated operating policies and compare them with the reference operation.
  6. Test the results on separate periods and scenarios.

A statistical dependency is at first a hypothesis, not proof of causality. This statement belongs visibly in the graphic.

Evaluation principle

Coordination potential = result of the coordinated alternative − result of the reference operation

The comparison uses the same boundary conditions and only the information that was available at decision time. A comparison with perfect knowledge of the future must be labeled explicitly as a theoretical upper bound.

Possible result measures

  • Contribution margin
  • Avoidable curtailment
  • Hydrogen demand coverage
  • Storage stress
  • Benefit and extra cost per party

Deliverables

Dependency model, constraints, reference operation, comparison scenarios and a potential report with assumptions, uncertainties and sensitivities.

Concrete procedures and tests (for example significance tests, causal confirmation, optimization procedures) are method proposals. They appear as binding roadmap content only after technical agreement; until then they appear at most in small type, labeled "proposal".

Decided (D20): T and L are equal execution variants.

  • T: tightly coupled, one process, reference execution.
  • L: loosely coupled, coordination through data timestamps, configurable permitted lag; building blocks can run with different owners.
  • The same building block code runs in both variants.
  • Binding test: L without allowed delay delivers the same result as T, bit for bit.

2 · Causal chain as a short story

  1. The hydrogen offtaker closes the valve.
  2. The pressure in the line rises. The buffer lasts about one more hour.
  3. The electrolyzer has to curtail.
  4. Its schedule is violated.
  5. The direct marketer has to sell more power on the market.

The point: the twin warns at step 2 and shows what a countermeasure does.

3 · Building blocks of the twin

  • Wind: wind turbine, wake between turbines, park controller
  • Measurement and balance: weather, meter, balance node, self-consumer
  • Storage and hydrogen: battery, electrolyzer, H₂ line, H₂ offtaker
  • Market and control: power market, direct marketer, human at the controls, optimizer
  • Operating rules: grid restrictions, field work, bat protection shutdowns

Every component has the same anatomy: state, displays, behavior and an exchangeable operating policy (schedule, human or optimizer).

4 · Stages with decision points

  1. A · Data and potential: Connect data, estimate the added value roughly. Without simulation.
  2. B · Shadow operation: The twin runs alongside and compares plan and reality.
  3. C · Cause and effect: Confirm causal chains with real operating data.
  4. D · Optimization: Propose a better operating policy and prove the extra revenue.

After every stage a visible gate: go on or stop.

5 · Test criteria view A only

  • Repeatability: same plan, same inputs, same interventions → bit-identical run.
  • Test L(0) = T, bit for bit.
  • Balances closed: power at the connection and hydrogen mass in the line.
  • Causal chain S1 in the right order, with timestamps.
  • Every key figure shows how much of it is measured and how much estimated.

6 · P9.6 Research "Digital Twins on Logs"

The choice between T and L is not open. The research questions are open:

  • Error bounds with allowed delay
  • Conservation of balance quantities
  • Order of cause and effect
  • Repeatability without transactions in KafScale
  • Cost and fault tolerance
  • Implementation of all three operating modes in L

Visual language both views

  • Status through shape and label, color in addition: done: filled circle designed/concept: circle planned: dashed proposal: diamond core: filled diamond research: dotted. The colors of this page are a proposal, not a requirement.
  • One accent: only the twin gets the signal color.
  • Wiring plan look: standard building blocks, individual wiring. No photos, no 3D renderings.
  • Legibility: readable on a slide from 5 m away. At most 12 words per text block in the main view.
  • There is no fixed OpenTSx branding. Fonts and palette are free, as long as they look technical and calm and are the same in both views.

Short glossary

Time seriesMeasured values over time, for example wind speed every second.
Smart TopicA data stream that learns from other streams and delivers forecasts.
Reference operationToday's separate operational management that the comparison is made against.
Coordination potentialResult of a coordinated operating policy minus the result of the reference operation, under the same conditions.
Digital twinComputational model of the real plant. A decision in the model changes all affected figures the way it would happen in reality.
Building block (atom)Smallest element of the twin, for example an electrolyzer or a meter.
Wiring planDescription of which building blocks are connected, and how. Individual per plant.
T / LTightly coupled (one program in lockstep) or loosely coupled (distributed parts that work together through data streams).
Decision pointThe end of a stage. Is the next step worth it?
