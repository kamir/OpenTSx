## Messaging Framework

### Core Value Propositions

**For Software Engineers:**
- "Master time series analysis in production Java applications"
- "From zero to production-ready in 6 hours"
- "Real-world patterns, not just theory"

**For Data Scientists:**
- "Bring your R/Python skills to enterprise Java"
- "Scale your analysis from laptop to cluster"
- "Bridge the gap between research and production"

**For Organizations:**
- "Onboard time series developers in days, not months"
- "Battle-tested patterns from production systems"
- "Free, open-source, community-driven"

### Key Differentiators

1. **Dual Track Approach** — Tailored to background (SWE vs TSx)
2. **Hands-On Exercises** — 21 practical exercises with solutions
3. **Production Focus** — Real-world patterns, not toy examples
4. **Automated Validation** — Test your knowledge as you learn
5. **Complete Infrastructure** — Docker Compose, testing, documentation
