## 📚 Specialized Deep-Dive Modules

After completing your primary journey, dive deeper into specific topics:

### 🔬 [Algorithm Deep Dives](04-ALGORITHM-DEEP-DIVES.md)
**Master the Mathematics and Theory**

**Modules:**
1. **Detrended Fluctuation Analysis (DFA)**
   - Theory: Long-range correlations and scaling
   - Mathematics: Polynomial detrending
   - Practice: Financial time series analysis
   - Exercise: Analyze stock market data

2. **Multifractal Detrended Fluctuation Analysis (MFDFA)**
   - Theory: Multifractal formalism
   - Mathematics: Generalized Hurst exponent
   - Practice: Turbulence and complex systems
   - Exercise: Climate data multifractal analysis

3. **Event Synchronization**
   - Theory: Synchronized dynamics
   - Mathematics: Event detection and timing
   - Practice: Climate teleconnections
   - Exercise: Brain signal synchronization

4. **Return Interval Statistics (RIS)**
   - Theory: Extreme events and risk
   - Mathematics: Distribution analysis
   - Practice: Earthquake recurrence
   - Exercise: Financial risk modeling

5. **Granger Causality**
   - Theory: Causal inference in time series
   - Mathematics: Vector autoregression
   - Practice: Economic indicators
   - Exercise: Trading signal causality

**Time:** 2-4 hours per module
